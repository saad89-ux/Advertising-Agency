# Brightleaf Skincare: Claims Register (SYNTHETIC)

Numbered entries. Anything not listed here is unknown and goes to a person.

## BL-C-01 [FORBIDDEN]
- Phrases: clinically proven, reduces wrinkles in 14 days, proven to reduce wrinkles
- Rule: No claim of clinical proof or timed wrinkle results.

## BL-C-02 [FORBIDDEN]
- Phrases: miracle, heals, cures
- Rule: No cure, heal or miracle language.

## BL-C-03 [FORBIDDEN]
- Phrases: dermatologist recommended, doctor recommended
- Rule: No endorsement claims.

## BL-C-04 [ALLOWED]
- Phrases: visibly smoother-looking skin
- Rule: Approved appearance claim.

## BL-C-05 [ALLOWED_WITH_FOOTNOTE]
- Phrases: dermatologist tested
- Rule: Footnote must appear in the ad text.
- Required footnote: `*Tested on 50 volunteers.`

## BL-C-06 [OFFER_MATCH]
- Phrases: n/a
- Rule: An offer in the ad must match the landing page, including price and end date.

## BL-C-07 [ALLOWED]
- Phrases: bestselling
- Rule: Allowed; sales data is checked monthly by the client.

# Brightleaf Skincare: Brand Book (SYNTHETIC)

## BL-BB-1.1 Voice
Warm, calm and expert. Short sentences. Talk about how skin looks, not what it treats.

## BL-BB-2.1 Headlines
Title Case. Five words or fewer.

## BL-BB-2.3 Headlines
No exclamation marks in headlines.

## BL-BB-3.1 Offers
State the offer exactly as the landing page shows it, including the end date if one is shown.

# Calmleaf Supplements: Claims Register (SYNTHETIC)

Numbered entries. Anything not listed here is unknown and goes to a person.

## CL-C-01 [FORBIDDEN]
- Phrases: doctors love it, doctor recommended, doctor approved
- Rule: No doctor endorsement.

## CL-C-02 [FORBIDDEN]
- Phrases: cures insomnia, treats insomnia
- Rule: No treatment or cure claims.

## CL-C-03 [ALLOWED_WITH_FOOTNOTE]
- Phrases: supports a restful night, supports better sleep
- Rule: Footnote must appear in the ad text.
- Required footnote: `*Not evaluated by the FDA.`

## CL-C-04 [FORBIDDEN]
- Phrases: guaranteed results, guaranteed to work
- Rule: No guarantees.

## CL-C-05 [FORBIDDEN]
- Phrases: 100% safe, completely safe
- Rule: No safety absolutes.

## CL-C-06 [OFFER_MATCH]
- Phrases: n/a
- Rule: An offer in the ad must match the landing page.

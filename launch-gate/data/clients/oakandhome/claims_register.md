# Oak & Home: Claims Register (SYNTHETIC)

Numbered entries. Anything not listed here is unknown and goes to a person.

## OH-C-01 [FORBIDDEN]
- Phrases: lowest price, best price guaranteed
- Rule: No price superlatives.

## OH-C-02 [CONDITIONAL]
- Phrases: handmade
- Rule: 'Handmade' only for products flagged handmade (shelves, tables).

## OH-C-03 [CONDITIONAL]
- Phrases: free delivery
- Rule: Free delivery must state the threshold and it must match the landing page.

## OH-C-04 [FORBIDDEN]
- Phrases: eco-friendly, planet-friendly
- Rule: Unqualified environmental claims are not allowed.

## OH-C-05 [ALLOWED_WITH_FOOTNOTE]
- Phrases: sustainably sourced
- Rule: Footnote must appear in the ad text.
- Required footnote: `*FSC certified timber.`

## OH-C-06 [OFFER_MATCH]
- Phrases: n/a
- Rule: An offer in the ad must match the landing page.
